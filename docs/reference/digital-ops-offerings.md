# Digital Ops Packages — Dashboard / Digital Assistant / AI Operations

> Source doc was originally titled "AI Automation Offerings." Renamed to **Digital Ops** as the umbrella category — see roadmap.md §5. Tier names below are unchanged.

*Custom digital systems for businesses that want to work smarter.*

Digital Ops is the operational side of the studio: tools, dashboards, assistants, integrations, and AI-powered workflows.

## Dashboard — $750+
*See your business.*

- Custom business dashboard
- One primary data source for the starting tier
- KPI cards, charts, and basic filters
- Responsive interface
- Deployment
- 1 revision round

**Examples:** Sales, operations, marketing, customer success, project tracking, inventory, reporting, security/threat intelligence.

## Digital Assistant — $1,200+
*Ask your business.*

- Custom AI assistant designed around the business
- Custom instructions and business context
- Knowledge/document integration where appropriate
- Defined assistant workflows
- Basic automation
- Deployment + testing
- Basic handoff documentation

**Examples:** Internal knowledge assistant, FAQ assistant, document assistant, lead qualification assistant, customer-facing helper.

## AI Operations — $3,500+
*Automate your business.*

- End-to-end operational workflow design
- AI-powered processing and decision steps
- Multiple connected tools or systems as scoped
- APIs / databases / automation integrations as needed
- Notifications, routing, reporting, or follow-up steps
- Testing + deployment
- Handoff documentation

**Examples:** Lead intake, customer requests, reporting, document processing, support triage, CRM workflows, security workflows, recurring operational tasks.

## Pricing + scope rules

- All Digital Ops pricing is a starting price. Final pricing is confirmed after the project is scoped.
- Dashboard complexity is driven primarily by data sources, data cleanup, integrations, user roles, refresh requirements, and custom logic.
- Digital Assistant pricing increases with knowledge sources, integrations, authentication, automation, and custom interfaces.
- AI Operations pricing increases with workflow complexity, number of connected systems, AI processing, APIs, data handling, and required reliability.
- Third-party subscriptions, API usage, hosting, model usage, and other service fees are paid by the client unless otherwise agreed.
- Anything outside the agreed scope is quoted separately before work begins.
