# Service Rules — Internal Reference

*NODE / NETWORK / SYSTEM. Powers the FAQ section and order-form confirmation copy — see roadmap.md.*

## 1. Payment

- 50% deposit is required to begin the project.
- Remaining 50% is due before the website goes live.
- Example: NODE at $500 = $250 to begin + $250 before launch.

## 2. Revisions

- NODE includes 1 revision round.
- NETWORK includes 2 revision rounds.
- SYSTEM includes 2 revision rounds.
- A revision round means one consolidated list of requested changes, not individual edits counted separately.

## 3. Client content + assets

- Client provides final business information, text, images, logos, contact details, social links, and other required assets unless otherwise agreed.
- The studio may format and organize supplied content, but content creation is not automatically included.

## 4. Timeline

- Typical project timeline: 1–3 weeks.
- The timeline begins once the required content/assets and initial payment have been received.
- Delays in receiving client materials may extend the project timeline.

## 5. Domain + hosting

- Client owns and maintains their domain and hosting accounts.
- The studio can assist with setup, connection, configuration, and deployment.

## 6. Scope changes

- Anything outside the selected package is quoted separately before work begins.
- New functionality, additional pages, or major changes may require an updated project quote.

## 7. Third-party services

- Client is responsible for subscription, hosting, domain, payment-processing, booking, newsletter, email, and other third-party service costs.
- The studio can help configure these services when included in the project scope.
